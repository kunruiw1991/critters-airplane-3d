## 📱 How to Install as a Full-Screen 3D App on iPad

1. Open **[https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-airplane-3d/](https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-airplane-3d/)** in **Safari** on your iPad.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with upward arrow in the top bar).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add** — the **Smiling Critters 3D Airplane Adventure (✈️)** icon will appear on your iPad Home Screen and cache all 3D models, Three.js WebGL engine, and custom character artwork for offline play!
